    Having something made

    Most of what I sew starts with someone's idea: a quilt for a new baby, a tote in a favorite color, a cover sized to a well-loved Bible.

    1. Tell me what you have in mind

      Send a request with what you'd like, the colors you love, and when you need it.

    2. We settle the details

      I'll write back with fabric ideas, size, timing and a price before any fabric gets cut.

    3. I get sewing

      Your piece is cut, pieced and quilted by hand, start to finish.

    4. It comes home with you

      We arrange pickup or delivery once it's finished.
